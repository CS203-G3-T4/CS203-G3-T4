About our data

Which numbers are measured, which are modelled, and where each one comes from.

Wholesale electricity price (USEP) Live, provisional

The Uniform Singapore Energy Price is set for every half-hour of the day. Wattly reads it from the free NEMS API, an unofficial community service with no uptime guarantee. When it is down, Wattly keeps showing the last price it saved and marks it as stale.

Live prices are provisional. The Energy Market Company publishes final prices a few business days later (EMC NEMS prices). USEP is a wholesale signal, not your full tariff.

Your household's electricity use Modelled, not metered

There is no public way to read a home's live smart-meter data, so Wattly models it:

  • We start from EMA's average monthly use for your home type (table below).
  • We subtract the appliances you listed, because we place those at their own times. EV charging is not in EMA's averages, so it is added on top and shown separately.
  • The rest is spread across the day with a typical Singapore pattern: aircon at night, a morning bump, a quiet weekday afternoon and an evening peak. Weekends use 10% more per day. This daily pattern is our assumption, not published data.
  • An appliance run three times a week counts as 3/7 of a run every day, so the numbers are averages.

Over a whole month, the base load plus your appliances adds up exactly to EMA's figure.

Appliances You enter them

Power is in kilowatts (how hard an appliance draws) and run time in minutes. Energy per run is power × hours, in kilowatt-hours. Your cost is energy × price.

Weather Live forecast

Daily temperature forecasts come from the National Environment Agency through data.gov.sg. A hot-day hint is a temperature signal, not a price prediction.

Households in the admin view Simulated

Apart from a handful of demo homes, the households in the admin view are generated, not real people. Their home-type mix roughly follows Singapore's housing stock and 70% are on price-linked plans, both of which are demo assumptions. They are always labelled "simulated" and cannot be used to log in.

Savings Assumption

Because Wattly cannot see your meter, an accepted suggestion is treated as followed. Savings are labelled "estimated" until the actual half-hourly prices are known.
