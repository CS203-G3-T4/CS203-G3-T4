Independent review of unusual prices

Review the historical prices before seeing statistical-rule decisions. Every half-hour starts unreviewed. These are revised final prices, not a reconstruction of what was known live.

This workbook contains no model forecasts or rule thresholds. Human price-anomaly labels do not prove a physical shortage or market cause. Do not use the separate rule-audit file while labelling. Team approval is still a separate decision.

Blue line: actual price in SGD/MWh. Click a point or a table row to inspect its preceding same-slot prices.

Consider whether an ordinary evening peak explains the price. Use uncertain rather than forcing a decision. Record the reason for each label.
