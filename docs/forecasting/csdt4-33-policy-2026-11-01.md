# CSDT4-33: price anomaly labels and relevant personal warnings

Policy ID: `SAME_SLOT_K3_RELEVANT_WARNING_V1`. Owner-authorised proposal, 1 November 2026. This fixes the definition for one bounded validation; it does not assert Jonathan/team sign-off or change the running app.

## Observed-price definition

Use the preceding 28 daily values for the same Singapore half-hour, available by the assessment time. Require at least 14 observations. Typical price is the median; spread is `max(1 SGD/MWh, 1.4826 × MAD)`; a statistical spike is strictly above `typical + 3 × spread`. Keep the current cheap/normal/expensive/spike display and deviation from typical. An ordinary evening peak is judged against its own evening history. One half-hour can count. Unavailable/stale/unverified inputs cannot support a confident current-price assessment. Missing and uncertain labels are never normal negatives.

This is an unusual-price definition, not physical scarcity proof or a Gaussian probability. k=3 is the fixed starting point, not a measured optimum. Genuine-label agreement remains a separate human review endpoint.

## Personal future-warning proposal

1. The existing price-only AI's frozen first eligible appliance plan predicts a spike during the usual run (`aiWarning`), proposes a feasible future alternative and satisfies the existing top-three/ownership constraints.
2. Estimated avoidable whole-run wholesale cost is at least S$0.10. This is a warning gate, not a change to the live S$0.05 recommendation policy.
3. The saved, issue-time empirical savings range is available, has at least 20 completed earlier matching days and has a strictly positive lower bound. Never fill a missing range from later outcomes. This is a savings-risk safeguard, not a probability that a spike will occur.
4. Group overlapping or adjacent affected usual-run intervals within a household into one warning episode. Count a run only once; repeated model origins do not multiply notifications. Refresh existing advice for material changes. The frozen first-plan replay cannot grade later notification updates, so it reports initial-episode proxies only.
5. Before adequate evidence exists, keep uncertain predictions informational. A forecast is described as possible/predicted; fresh observed high prices may receive the statistical current-price label immediately.

The product target is at most two false personal-warning episodes out of ten. A false spike-warning episode has no actual statistical spike in the affected usual-run periods. Separately report actual savings, harmful moves/loss and episodes whose proposed move did not save money. The joint useful-warning endpoint requires both an actual spike and a cheaper proposed run. Human-meaningful precision remains unavailable without reviewed labels.

## Fixed validation and acceptance limits

Compare one candidate (the S$0.10 plus positive-range gate above) with the unchanged AI warning-plan control on identical first-plan exposures. No fitting, threshold grid, reranking, additional feed, model promotion, live notice, trial rewrite or timer activation. B3 may be reported as context on identical forecast pairs, not used to select the candidate after seeing outcomes.

Report raw run-target pairs separately from deduplicated fixed-lead targets, observed-rule flags, warning episodes and appliance plans. Report missed events and false-warning duration alongside precision; preserve pending truth and censored boundaries. Zero warnings give undefined precision, not 100% accuracy. A seven-day batch may contain too little support. Require at least 20 warning episodes across 20 distinct affected market periods and 20 fully observed actual spike events before considering a descriptive target result supported; this minimum is not a statistical guarantee. Show a confidence interval only with its dependence limitations. No automatic acceptance or promotion occurs, even on a supported result.

Newly accumulated live outcomes after the previous numeric assessment can be examined once as a preliminary extension. They are not a fully blind confirmation: today's chart/current forecast has already been viewed and this policy is being specified afterwards. Reserve the next half-hour onwards for one seven-day prospective cohort, then grade it once after a further 13 hours for full 12-hour target completion and normal collection lag. Stop after that batch; insufficient support remains insufficient rather than causing an automatic extension/search.

The existing price-only model, trial identity/dates/policy, current k=3 rule and active recommendation/alert settings remain frozen. CSDT4-33 stays In Progress pending measured support and team sensitivity acceptance. CSDT4-94 remains Done on its documented owner-accepted statistical scope.
